I read this book in two sittings flat, entirely entranced. Mester’s debut memoir about consumerism in her family—her hoarder grandma, her over-shopping father—connect seemingly disparate journeys of retail jobs & retail therapy & weight-loss summer camps. All families have unique relationships with shopping/consumption, and many of us assume our family has a “normal” one, but this book convinced me there’s always more layers to unpack.
