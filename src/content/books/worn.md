Fashion gets analysis/attention for its cultural impact, but *Worn* uses a less expected, more intriguing lens: the materials of our clothing. Through each basic material (cotton, linen, silk, polyester, etc), it mixes stories of labor, culture, supply chain, and environmental histories that I found thoroughly fascinating.
