The book that started it all: I read this in fourth grade, and its breakdown of the flow of physical goods was indelibly imprinted in my mind: extraction, production (toxics in, toxics out), distribution (the golden arrow), consumption, disposal. Its stories of the Bhopal industrial disaster, the rampant use of toxic chemicals in consumer products, and the variety of bad disposal options for all objects forever changed my relationship with material goods.
